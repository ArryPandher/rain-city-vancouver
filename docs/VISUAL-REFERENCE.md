# Vancouver art direction — 2026-11-14

User requested GTA VI as the visual reference and rejected the earlier generic city appearance.

Reference: [Rockstar Games — official GTA VI screenshot gallery](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media/screenshots), specifically Jason Duval 02, Vice City 03, Leonida Keys 02 and 03. Reviewed the actual full-resolution images; Rockstar imagery is reference only and is not distributed with this game.

The useful visual cues are layered foreground/middle-distance/background; warm direct light with cool, readable shade; restrained highlights; deep window recesses and physically varied façades; individually recognizable building silhouettes; vegetation that frames streets; and small accumulated signs of use around curbs, doors and street furniture. Realism comes from coherent scale and material response, not bloom alone.

Translate those cues to Vancouver: real landmark geometry, photographed heritage masonry and windows, a real North Shore horizon, coastal glass towers with balconies, trolley wires, Gastown lamps and brick paving, and pedestrian-scale storefronts. Keep sunlit sail fabric white, glass reflective, concrete rough, and asphalt granular. The updated goal prioritizes ordinary daylight gameplay, with no new photo-mode work. Check the player's walking/driving camera and motion, not a special cinematic camera used to hide unfinished areas.

GTA VI remains the visual benchmark; real Vancouver photographs determine actual
architecture, materials and scene relationships. Later user-supplied GTA-style
screenshots are additional visual direction, not independently authenticated
Rockstar source images. Use the official gallery above when identifying GTA VI
reference work. Priority scenes and review constraints are in
[Daylight landmarks](DAYLIGHT-LANDMARKS.md).

## Daylight comparison applied to the current pass

Re-reviewed the cached official **Leonida Keys 02** still and reference contact
sheet against the actual 0c1bea8 car/Harbour/gallery gameplay captures. The
transferable standard is connected ground construction, real window and trim
depth, material readable in shade, restrained highlights and wear concentrated
at plausible edges. These reference stills do not establish gameplay frame rate.

The gallery comparison led to a bounded rear-wing reconstruction: the source
already had shallow window recesses, but thick pale crosses, identical row
heights and missing sill profiles flattened them. A real Vancouver rear view
supports darker sash frames, shorter upper windows, projecting stone sills,
rusticated base courses and four engaged entrance columns. Keep that evidence
separate from the GTA material/lighting benchmark; see
[the gallery source note](sources/gallery-facade.md) for the exact photograph
and remaining approximations.

Canada Place's blank street-facing base and inaccessible promenade are still a
larger composition problem than its mast detail. The official front-plaza plan
establishes the elevated public approach; its local height integration remains
unfinished. Neither the new gallery detail nor the corrected chase camera
closes the larger gaps in character realism, animation, street coherence,
vegetation, water or sustained frame rate.

This remains an independent browser prototype. The reference establishes the direction; it is not a claim of matching Rockstar's production fidelity, animation, simulation or content depth.
