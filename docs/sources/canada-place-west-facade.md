# Canada Place west exhibition-hall concourse

2026-11-14. The current candidate replaces the earlier flat two-storey window grid with one continuous stepped glazed envelope along the five source SAILS west edges (103.10 m). This is a reference-grounded architectural approximation, not an as-built reconstruction. Native walking, driving, cycling and close oblique review passed; see ../CANADA-PLACE-CONCOURSE.md for measured results and limits.

The five mapped edges form one connected longitudinal envelope. The highest glazed band occupies a broad central region, stations22–81.10 m; opaque upper fascias reduce the repeated glass pattern toward the ends. Those station limits are interpretive choices, not a photographed complete elevation schedule. The highest quarter-round is now replaced by an upright solid fascia in the two end regions. The lower two curved tiers, ground footprint and maximum height remain unchanged. The22/81.10m divisions are still explicitly estimated: neither the photograph nor the aerial resolves their exact mast-registered locations. This is a bounded section correction, not a completed as-built longitudinal height schedule. Projection eases to65% of central depth (9.36 m) over the first/last15 m. Exact hotel/end-hall junction geometry remains approximate; the separate hotel canopy is not modified.

Ground glazing, closed door reading, curve-following frames, sill, upper terrace and opaque backing remain exterior-only. The source mass is not opened as an interior. The existing rigging is still roof-only: ground stay-anchor plinths and their real full-height stay arrangement are **not reconstructed by this pass**. This is a substantial remaining fidelity limitation; the envelope must not be described as completing the mast/stay system.
