# Canada Place west exhibition-hall concourse

2026-11-14. The current candidate replaces the earlier flat two-storey window grid with one continuous stepped glazed envelope along the five source SAILS west edges (103.10 m). This is a reference-grounded architectural approximation, not an as-built reconstruction. Native walking, driving, cycling and close oblique review passed; see ../CANADA-PLACE-CONCOURSE.md for measured results and limits.

## Current geometric interpretation

Local deck7.8, ground door head10.75, upper promenade17.65. Three nominal quarter-round rises are2.3 m; the upper lip is7.9 m outside the simplified hall wall, giving an initially traced central outer glass projection14.8 m. The staged candidate contracts all horizontal profile depths by2.7%, to14.4 m outer projection and approximately7.69 m upper lip, preserving more than0.95 m public-route clearance including the collision system’s0.55 m safety margin. All are explicit drawing-ratio/model-datum estimates. Source SAILS boundaries are treated as the hall wall, **not** mistaken for the upper lip.

The five mapped edges form one connected longitudinal envelope. The highest glazed band occupies a broad central region, stations22–81.10 m; opaque upper fascias reduce the repeated glass pattern toward the ends. Those station limits are interpretive choices, not a photographed complete elevation schedule. All three curved tier heights currently remain fixed along the strip: the end treatment changes upper material and horizontal projection, **not the actual longitudinal tier-height variation visible in photographs**. That height variation is unfinished and must not be claimed as achieved. Projection eases to65% of central depth (9.36 m) over the first/last15 m. Closed glazed end sections reveal the tiered silhouette instead of introducing15 m blank opaque return walls. Exact hotel/end-hall junction geometry remains approximate; the separate hotel canopy is not modified.

Ground glazing, closed door reading, curve-following frames, sill, upper terrace and opaque backing remain exterior-only. The source mass is not opened as an interior. The existing rigging is still roof-only: ground stay-anchor plinths and their real full-height stay arrangement are **not reconstructed by this pass**. This is a substantial remaining fidelity limitation; the envelope must not be described as completing the mast/stay system.

`canada-place-concourse-data.json` records the section profile, longitudinal station positions/normals/scales, local footprint and world footprint. Parent collision integration must block `worldFootprint` through worldy17.73, replacing the obsolete assumption that all new frontage remains inside the old SAILS polygon. `CANADA_PLACE_CONCOURSE_FOOTPRINTS` re-exports it for callers. Footprint includes120 mm outer frame allowance. No source pier, wall, sail, mast or hotel vertex is moved.
