# West Canadian Trail railing refinement

The inspected [TourbyTransit dry-day west-trail photograph](https://cdn.tourbytransit.com/vancouver/images/Canadian-Trail-at-Canada-Place.jpg) and [Brenco's March 14, 2023 fabricator photograph](https://www.brenco.com/images/whats-happening/Salish-Sea--the-Journey-Within-1678831506.jpg), from [its project article](https://www.brenco.com/whats-happening/salish-sea-the-journey-within), show pale painted metal rails with framed, open wire-panel infill, layered horizontal rails and a low toe strip. References are locally inspected photographs, not game textures. The former is archival; the latter is wet and does not establish dry gloss. Neither supplies surveyed sections, pitch or spacing.

`canada-place-west-railing.ts` adds one bounded mesh along the existing source west edge (approximately 308 m). Source concrete deck geometry, collision plane and all source rail vertices remain unchanged; only the original 12 top-bar triangles are removed. The original top rail is replaced by a rounded handrail; original posts receive painted covers and grounded base plates. Lower rails and recessed fine wire infill create open panels rather than an opaque wall. Handrail radius 50 mm, post covers 120 mm, 220 mm base plates, approximately 160 mm wire spacing and 6 mm wires are explicitly estimated visual dimensions. Source post spacing is preserved, not asserted as the real construction spacing.

The exact reviewed Codriver CC-BY-4.0 asset is `public/models/vancouver/canada-place.glb`, SHA-256 `1b7eab323b7bb3aa242438c2518910a3d88fae170bef74d31408bcb8c5096cbe`. Within its steel primitive, vertices 0–23 are the west top rail and 24–1703 are 70 west post boxes. A position/index fingerprint of only this bounded source range permits independent entrance and mast repairs while rejecting changes to the west edge. It does not select geometry with a broad spatial box. Source credits remain in [canada-place.json](canada-place.json).

Loader API: `refineCanadaPlaceWestRailing(bakedSteelGeometry, originalMaterialName)` returns a geometry or null. It may run after the gate and mast repairs. Add its mesh to the same local landmark group with an existing shared pale painted-metal material, receiveShadow enabled, **castShadow disabled**. Retained original posts provide structural shadows; fine infill intentionally does not create dense aliasing wire shadows on the paving. One additional material batch remains below 30,000 triangles and needs no textures, lamps, probes or runtime updates. The finer geometric grid can still become subpixel at distance; native camera acceptance remains necessary.

Tests load the actual quantized GLB and pin its SHA-256; verify exact preservation of all source positions and the ordered subsequence of every non-top-bar index after existing gate/mast edits; check count budget, entry polygon exclusion, grounded bases and narrow edge-strip containment; sample the existing route for player-radius clearance; and reject modified components/repeated or wrong-material calls. TypeScript checks pass. These checks establish geometry safety, not final native visual acceptance.
