# Howe Street asset research

Research on 2026-11-15 did not establish a downloadable, high-detail and clearly
redistributable replacement for the Fairmont Waterfront or PwC street frontages.
Viewing a model or service is not evidence of permission to put its contents in
this public repository.

- [City LiDAR 2022](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/lidar-2022/) is
  covered by the [Open Government Licence–Vancouver](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/).
  Published metadata reports 49 points/m² and 0.081 m vertical accuracy. The
  relevant grid tile is `491000_5459000`; its individual archive size and actual
  points have not been verified. It is a potential measured geometry/grade
  source, not a textured facade kit. No point-cloud archive was downloaded.
- The public [Esri Vancouver 3D scene](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=9813c29e84994c8a80ab729e865528e5)
  is subject to Esri licensing. The linked
  [licence summary](https://goto.arcgis.com/termsofuse/viewsummary) does not permit
  treating the hosted content as a downloadable, redistributable game asset.
- The Sketchfab [Canada Place architectural feature](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/canada-place-architectural-feature-7b0dcfdf67144ccc966595f60d0c7ba6)
  lists 49,999 faces and CC BY-NC. It is one feature, not the street scene, and
  its noncommercial restriction does not fit the project's broadly reusable
  asset pipeline. It was not acquired.
- A [whole-Vancouver model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/vancouver-bc-canada-593091935a59471198d0ea95f9f54305)
  advertises CC BY and about 2.124 million faces, but supplies insufficient
  provenance and no verified close-range facade evidence. Archive and texture
  payload remain unverified. It was not acquired.

The practical path remains original source-grounded architecture with compatible
licensed materials and components. This is a bounded search result, not proof
that no suitable asset exists anywhere. No purchase, account creation or asset
download occurred in this audit.
