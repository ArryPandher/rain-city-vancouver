## Why current skyline identity drifts

`public/data/vancouver.json` contains the three parent ways with raw levels 15 (1199), 31 (1177), and 27 (Carina), but **none of the five upper-part IDs listed above**. `loadCity()` preserves raw existing levels while merging refreshed metadata. `architecture.ts` then uses a seeded random high-rise facade style for these parents; none has a dedicated building handler or a matching entry in `vancouver-landmarks.json`. `buildingHeight()` derives heights from levels where no explicit height exists. Carina's terrace classification also selects house-related treatments. The problem is missing part hierarchy and identified facade composition, not simply insufficient procedural detail.

Next action: start with 1199, retrieve/retain its two cached upper outlines, verify their overlap and vertical extent, and author the narrow reflective banded tower over the existing site datum. Use the original park photograph plus the inspected owner elevation. Then reconstruct 1177's dark tower/podium contrast. Carina needs a verified park-facing elevation before deciding detailed balcony placement. Keep a fixed ordinary-play View A capture and walking approach for review; no present photo match or surveyed reconstruction is claimed.
