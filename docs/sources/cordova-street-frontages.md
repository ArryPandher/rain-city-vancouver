2026-11-15. Source-guided exterior work at 325 Howe and the adjoining Terminal City Club extension. These are original modeled interpretations for the playable street, not scans or a claim of photographic fidelity.

These proportions and profile depths are photographic interpretations; the photographs do not provide surveyed dimensions. ## Native findings and final candidate corrections

The native256px combined pass visibly showed the raised frontage, rail, street fixtures and revised club tiers. Ordinary walking entered the open Howe ramp at y8.82, reached y8.94 mid-ramp and the y9.1578 landing, then turned onto Cordova without falling through or crossing the railing. Driving/braking and pistol15→14 were observed; the debug return safely exited the vehicle and restored normal walking with no recorded warning/error and null reflection error. These checks do not establish AAA fidelity or sustained smoothness.

The tight corner exposed the chase camera entering the character's head. A conservative near-camera capsule now temporarily hides the protagonist using the actual interpolated camera position, with a20cm release margin to prevent flicker. Camera position, collision, mode and FOV remain unchanged. The character and its shadow return once clear; the shadow is also absent during this brief hidden interval. Six camera tests include the recorded corner and restoration/aiming cases; native acceptance is still required for this correction.

The first crack-free pavement candidate showed broad repeating bands in the actual frame and was rejected. Its exact crop was regenerated with broad Gaussian variation removed in linear albedo/roughness and normalXY before mirroring, without amplifying fine aggregate. Source hashes and1.2m scale are unchanged. Decoded mean error is at most0.324% for albedo and0.024% roughness; three512px files total447,286bytes. Updated hashes and preparation statistics are committed with the source manifest. A native comparison is required before accepting this replacement.

The512px reflection candidate did not reach a successful native review: the existing tab stopped answering CDP while its renderer was busy. No specific cause is established. The subsequent candidate restored both local captures to256px, added a yield after each completed capture, and reloaded the same tab through the localhost preview alias; it reached playable startup. Because several conditions changed, this does not isolate resolution as the cause. Default captures therefore remain256px pending a separate verified higher-resolution comparison. The512 API support remains available, but its extra target payload is not used by this release.

The startup loop previously submitted all32 captures in one uninterrupted task (up to192 cube-face views and32 shadow passes before ready). It now updates visible count progress and yields only after each capture restores renderer/scene state. Capture order and completed imagery are unchanged. This improves scheduling opportunities; faster total loading and the cause of the earlier stall are not claimed.
