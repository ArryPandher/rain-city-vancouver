## Native glazing calibration

The initial IOR 1.5 candidate had a detailed, unobstructed native cube capture but nearly black head-on panes. A controlled local change to effective IOR 1.8 in both optical passes increases normal Fresnel reflectance from 4% to approximately 8.16%, with about 4.3% less normal transmitted backing contribution. All other material and probe settings stayed fixed. This is an authored approximation for multiple glazing interfaces, not measured bulk IOR or evidence of a particular coating.

Matched close and wide native comparisons showed modest additional blue/sky variation while retaining dark glazing and physical recess depth, without a new mirror-like sheen or glare. The adjustment was accepted. Reflected-city detail remains limited. An alternative face33 probe position was audited but not adopted: rays cleared the local building at both locations, and the original native capture already contained detailed surroundings. No additional capture was created. Native evidence is in artifacts/local/near-podium-{candidate,fresnel}-{close,wide}.png and near-podium-initial-probe.png.
