# 325 Howe: original gameplay exterior

2026-11-15. Exact-building replacement for `w143683962`, the P. Angela Webster Building, historically the Pacific Coast Fire Building, at Howe and Cordova. It replaces the unrelated repeated Gastown arch photograph and invented coffee frontage. [Identification](howe-325-howe-audit.md) and [photographic/City source fit](howe-325-howe-source-fit.md) preserve the evidence and uncertainty.

## Architecture and street contact

The modern photographic reference has **three Cordova bays and seven Howe bays**, with six upper rows plus the current street floor. The seven-bay count corrects the earlier approximate six. Cordova has pale stone spandrels; warm brick panels belong to Howe. Modeled details include continuous pale piers, grey base courses, paired rectangular sash windows, 0.34m upper/0.52m lower reveals, sash rails, projecting sills, closed entry doors, small handles and a modest present-day parapet. The lost heavy historical cornice is not restored.

Both public faces use the unchanged map footprint and horizontal building datum. The masonry base extends to local -1.3m to close against the shared sloping sidewalk. Two closed Howe entrance thresholds are sampled from the actual terrain plus 40mm, rather than sitting a metre above the street. This is exterior scenery, not a playable interior.

The main roof is flat, with one small inset plant enclosure following the source's 50.268m² rooftop part, plus restrained low service equipment. Native reference review raised the street base to 4.65m (belt 4.95m), tightened the six upper rows to 3.15m spacing and left a 1.39m top frieze. The inferred roof/parapet elevations are local 24.3m/24.792m at centroid datum 9.257773m. The historic 27.652m small-element maximum is an upper source control, not a building-wide solid roof. Source age, approximate map registration and the inferred `City - 7.52` vertical conversion are documented in the source-fit report. Floor proportions, current material condition and fine door details remain visual interpretations.

Geometry totals **15,248 triangles, 30,496 vertices and nine material batches**, including shallow closed window cavities and varied upper-sash blinds. The window-depth revision adds 4,506 triangles and two materials; exterior pane, reveal, sash and sill coordinates stay unchanged. The runtime generator does not replace or enlarge building collision. Exact-ID dispatch precedes generic heritage/storefront generation and owns its below-grade masonry base.

## Materials

Six existing CC0 images supply metric brick and joint-free mineral color/normal/roughness maps. Rob Tuytel's Red Brick and Charlotte Baglioni's Granite Tile processing and provenance remain in `public/textures/landmarks/SOURCES.json`. These are generic material scans, not scans of the real building. Texture sampler clones prevent changing neighboring materials. The panes now use one non-emissive dielectric reflection/attenuation pass over actual closed recesses. Each cavity has a back 0.878m behind the pane, four returns and no occupiable interior. Upper sashes have deterministic open or partially lowered blinds, covering 22–58% of selected openings; the reference supports variation but these individual positions are interpreted. Ground-floor recesses stay empty and closed.

The first clear-glass candidate (IOR1.5, roughness0.065) looked too dark in native gameplay. The revised building-only IOR2.3 is an **effective Fresnel fit**, not a measurement of the real glass or coating. Both the native BRDF and slab attenuation use this same value: at normal incidence with survival0.8, approximately22.7% reflected and58.0% transmitted energy, before scene lighting. Roughness0.045 reaches Three's minimum0.0525 and the sharpest256px capture level. No diffuse paint, emissive fill, second refraction render or changes to other lobby glass defaults are used.

Only this building and the Customs Warehouse opt into fixed256px static reflection captures; all other probes remain128px. More pixels improve angular detail but cannot fix positional error in a single capture shared across a facade. This remains an approximation, not ray tracing or exact window-by-window reflection transport. See [optics and weathering limits](howe-glazing-and-weathering.md).

Owner and Changing Vancouver photographs were inspected as architectural references only; neither is distributed as a runtime asset. The City 2022 aerial was used to distinguish the flat roof and small raised enclosure. No paid asset or external image-generation service was used.

## Verification

Geometry checks cover mapped footprint and bay hierarchy, every upper recess and sash pair, reveal normals/UVs, lower openings and door clearances, closed roof/parapet, finite geometry and bounded projections, datum translation, actual sloping street contact, external reflection-probe placement and exact dispatch. Added ray tests pass through the removed frame backing to the closed cavity and all four returns, and check partial blinds behind the original panes. Shader tests cover negative mapped coordinates and bounded reflected/transmitted energy for the building's effective Fresnel fit. TypeScript and production build pass. The local full suite passed598 tests before the final material calibration and its additional passing energy test; final CI is recorded with the release.

Native checks in the sole Codex browser tab reached the corner (452.333,8.324,-407.264) and closer Cordova view (438.39,8.43,-402.86), fired the pistol15→14, entered the summoned car, drove45km/h, braked to zero and exited. Day→night→day refresh preserved the two256px targets and valid nearby captures; the final daylight profile recorded53 cumulative captures and null reflection/car errors. Captured warnings/errors were empty. This was a short Cordova drive, not a complete Fairmont driveway loop.
