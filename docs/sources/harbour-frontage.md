# Bute stair flanking terrace frontage

2026-11-14. Bounded original exterior interpretation for the fountain backdrop. Source: Darwin Construction's [Harbour Green Park project](https://www.darwinconstruction.ca/projects/development/harbour-green-park-1), specifically [harbour_3_840x520.jpg](https://www.darwinconstruction.ca/assets/Projects/harbour_green/harbour_3_840x520.jpg), cached locally as `artifacts/local/harbour-green-reference/photo-4.jpg`. The photograph was inspected directly. It is historical reference, not a licensed game texture; no reference pixels are included in the build.

The source shows low stone flanking frontage, dark recessed openings, repeated projecting canopy members and balustrades around the central stair axis. The implementation interprets that rhythm in six shallow exterior bays, three per side, aligned to the mapped Bute middle landing and fountain-pier axis. No tenant names, advertising, interiors or claim of exact measured dimensions are added. Existing CC0 cast-concrete maps are reused.

The refinement compares this photograph directly with `artifacts/local/288-public-harbour-gameplay.jpg`. In that gameplay view the first horizontal slats nearly disappear, leaving plain dark kiosk-like boxes. The revised exterior uses a visibly lower seaward eave, grey sloping infill and dark ribs, plus thicker balustrade piers/caps. The source supports those profile and contrast cues; the chosen 1.30 m canopy run and 0.61 m rise remain authored dimensions rather than measured facts. Panels are opaque rough exterior material, with no transparency or indoor scene. The broader bay widths and placement remain unchanged to preserve the mapped circulation. Placement checks now include the expanded canopy overhang. No new texture or light is required.

The game terrain already interprets this area as a continuous slope. New walls extend below the local surface, while the roof rises above sampled corner grades. A wide central opening preserves the 27 mapped stair risers. The placement guard rejects complete bays that overlap mapped paths, stair/landing footprints, existing buildings or nearby tree trunks. No trees are removed. The roof is closed scenery, not a new walkable terrace. This deliberately avoids a wholesale remesh of the stair terrain in this pass.

Photographic roof heights, structural details, tenancy and present-day facade configuration have not been surveyed. This is a composition improvement candidate, not a matched-photo result. Parent integration must inspect it from pedestrian height and along the upper/middle connectors; withhold it if the apparent scale, grounding, tree interaction or correspondence to the reference is wrong. `tests/harbour-frontage.test.ts` checks route clearance, finite/bounded geometry, terrain intersection and conflict rejection; tests do not establish visual realism.

Integration: call `buildHarbourFrontage(parent, data, materials, collision)` after the harbour terrain is established and include returned meshes in the normal world chunk collection. No separate lights, reflection capture, textures, runtime loop or asset downloads are required.
