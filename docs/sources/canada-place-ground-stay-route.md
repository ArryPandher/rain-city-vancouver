# Ground-stay route and collision integration

The five ground-stay plinths use the exact visible chamfered base polygons exported by `canada-place-ground-stays-data.ts`. Their body dimensions and offsets are architectural estimates documented by the geometry workstream, not surveyed coordinates. The route does not invent a larger pier to accommodate them.

The first proposed 17.5 m anchor line with 2.8 m radial bodies left no walkable waterside gap against the actual source pier: its edge is only about 19.0–19.4 m from the source hall line at these stations. That proposal was rejected before route integration. The final visible bodies project to 15.90 m centrally/15.60 m at the ends, with a 2.4 m radial footprint partly under the independently confirmed glazed overhang. The route passes on the waterside, retaining existing source pier/rail containment checks and the entrance connector.

`CANADA_PLACE_DECK_ROUTE` retains its entrance/hotel approach and receives five waterside anchor-clearance nodes. Nodes lie 1.45 m outside the visible body's radial edge (1.50 m at the end anchors). Tests sample the full path every 0.1 m using the actual movement routine: player radius 0.36 m, bike 0.45 m, car centre 1.16 m and front/rear 0.9 m circles offset ±1.35 m along the heading, matching the gameplay controller. Minimum clearance beyond these car collision capsules is **0.270 m** against the plinths. These dimensions establish a drivable corridor, not permission to drive anywhere across the pedestrian deck or proof of high-speed cornering safety.

`canadaPlaceGroundStayAt` checks the exact exported polygons with the caller's physical radius; it does not apply the larger facade 0.55 m allowance. The body obstruction is height-gated to world 7.88–10.33 m. Queries above the body clear it; the same ground point remains at the original deck height and water classification. This is a conservative footprint prism of the concrete body, not collision on individual thin wires or articulated cable fittings.

Concourse collision now distinguishes the ground glazing footprint from the outer roof envelope. Below the source door-head 10.75 m plus group offset 0.08 m, `solidAt` uses `groundWorldFootprint`; at or above 10.83 m it uses the existing upper footprint. Ordinary ground actors can occupy the backed pier beneath the glazed overhang, while the recessed wall and overhead remain closed. Existing hotel/hall masses, old pier below the public level, retaining side-entry blocks and source pier/rail margins remain in place. No broad water exemption was introduced.

Validation: ten collision tests, five actual-GLB deck tests (521 route/grid support rays), entry-seam checks and TypeScript checks pass. Material/geometry-specific tests in the facade and ground-stay suites verify the corresponding visible surfaces. Native walking/driving and camera review remains the parent task's acceptance step. Queries are position/radius/reference-height tests, not full vertical character capsules; unusual airborne head contact under the soffit remains a broader controller limitation.

The loader activates these ground stays when the source-facade preflight succeeds, replacing the obsolete west roof sockets and chords while retaining the east roof stays and all mast shafts. The plinths use dedicated 512 px CC0 mineral maps totalling 90,894 bytes, prepared from a joint- and tie-hole-free concrete crop with restrained normal detail and ground-exposure shading. See [the ground-stay geometry and material notes](canada-place-ground-stays.md) for provenance and limits.
