# Ironworkers Memorial Bridge: deck-truss correction

The previous generic truss renderer placed teal posts, diagonals and crossbars15m above the driving deck, producing a through-truss tunnel. The actual Ironworkers is a **deck-truss bridge**: its road is above the ochre/yellow steel. The driving skyline is open apart from roadside equipment and sign gantries. This is directly visible in the engineering/photo sources below.

`ironworkers-bridge.ts` replaces only Ironworkers' generic truss and repeated generic piers through a bridge-specific branch in `landmarks.ts`. It adds variable-depth main trusses below the deck, flanged chords/diagonals, transverse floor beams, lateral bracing, a deck underside/fascia and supports at structural bearings. Steel sole plates connect the cut ends of inclined members to flat pier caps. Shared panel nodes are emitted once. Two merged meshes use painted ochre steel and the existing concrete material; no downloaded photo is a runtime asset.

## Source evidence and limits

- [BC Ministry of Transportation, Ironworkers risk assessment,2018, section3](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/driving-and-transportation/reports-and-reference/reports-and-studies/lower-mainland/2018-03-16-ironworkers-risk-assessment.pdf):619m central steel assembly consisting of142m south anchor,335m main and142m north anchor spans; full structure including north approach1,292m.
- [SEABC/IABSE Vancouver Notable Structures,2017](https://www.seabc.ca/wordpress/files/notable_structures/Vancouver_Notable_Structures_IABSE_SEABC_2017.pdf): six highway lanes, two segregated pedestrian/cycle paths, four approximately86m north approach trusses and the three main spans.
- [Model Updating of the Ironworkers Memorial Second Narrows Bridge, Figure2/description](https://www.academia.edu/figures/17092284/figure-2-bent-south-end-of-the-approach-truss-spans-deck): approach truss depth13m, truss spacing14.6m, transverse beams at about10.5m. These establish structural proportions; this reconstruction is not an engineering model.
- [Webgeer / photo-tips.ca, June2005 photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ironworkers_Memorial_Bridge_Vancouver_BC.jpg), CC BY-SA2.5 with required attribution to photo-tips.ca: visible roadway over yellow steel, substantial flanged members, curved lower chord and concrete bearing piers. Research copy `artifacts/local/ironworkers-reference/webgeer-2005.jpg`; no image incorporated into runtime.
- [Graham, Ironworkers Memorial Bridge Improvements, completed September2015](https://www.grahambuilds.com/ironworkers-memorial-bridge-improvements/): sidewalks widened from1.2m to2.5m and new3m safety fence. This establishes that the existing sparse game rails remain approximate. Fence/sidewalk reconstruction is deferred because the current bridge collision does not represent those barriers; adding a visible fence alone would lack physical parity.

## Preserved gameplay and estimated registration

The original grade-reference line remains `[6806,-952]→[6776,-2268]`, length1,316.34m, width36m. The existing collision-owned endpoint/crest height function, asphalt ribbon, road lanes/markings, side rails, travel point and every other bridge remain unchanged. The creator receives the actual `CollisionWorld.bridgeHeight` callback, including its terrain-derived endpoint heights. The initial structure pass used12m deck segmentation; the subsequent shared alignment now provides matched top/underside rings and approach refinement. It supplies no duplicate top. See `ironworkers-alignment.md` for the road-envelope correction.

The current deck is an authored sinusoidal grade, not a surveyed profile. Mapping the documented sections naively from the southern endpoint would put the deep main truss below water near the low approach. This pass centres the335m main span at the existing crest, with142m anchor spans either side. Main assembly stations along the current line are348.671→490.671→825.671→967.671m. Four86m north approach spans follow, leaving a small plain terminal stub. The south approach remains a plain estimated structure. **These bearing locations are fitted to the existing scene, not surveyed geography or a claim that the real south approach has this layout.** Future exact registration must update visual and playable profiles together.

Main truss depth is estimated from the reference silhouette:23m at main bearings,7.8m at midspan and13m at anchor ends/approach junctions. Member sections, ochre paint value,42cm deck section and plain approach support spacing are explicit visual estimates. The14.6m primary truss spacing is retained, while transverse members extend beneath the existing36m gameplay deck; the game deck width has not been silently narrowed to source engineering dimensions.

## Verification and native acceptance

`tests/ironworkers-bridge.test.ts` checks documented main spans, continuous main/approach depth and actual ray contact between bearing shoes/pier caps; every generated steel vertex stays at least25cm below the live road (bearing shoes meet the underside of the42cm slab);495 driving-height upward rays remain clear;447 downward rays verify the single underside against the original grade within18mm; and an elevated/tilted callback preserves alignment without mutating bridge data. The initial straight structure had35,016 triangles. The road-aligned revision has41,796 triangles,96 panels and14 bearings in two merged meshes; its6,696-triangle asphalt top is separate. No full suite or build is part of this scoped verification.

Cached before views: `artifacts/local/86-ironworkers-road-drive.png` and `87-ironworkers-northbound.png` show the incorrect overhead teal tunnel during ordinary driving. They are historical baseline evidence, not current native acceptance. Root should drive northbound from the existing travel arrival, confirm the open sky and unchanged lane/grade contact, then inspect a side/reverse view for the gold underside silhouette and bearing continuity. Confirm quiet-frame performance and other bridges remain unchanged. No surveyed reconstruction or visual parity claim follows from unit tests alone.

## Road-aligned revision

`ironworkers-alignment.md` supersedes the straight-plan placement described above. The original line remains the grade reference, while all visible sections and support follow the preserved source road corridor. Main spans use true curved plan distance; their bearing locations remain fitted estimates, not surveyed positions. No safety fence has been added.
