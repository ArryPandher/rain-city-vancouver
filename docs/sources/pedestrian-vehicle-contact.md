# Pedestrian / vehicle contact

The native `canada-clarity-local-car.jpg` showed a standing NPC through the stopped car's rear-right roof/window area near (478.60, 7.88, -588.45). Summon placement previously received only vehicle occupancy; driving checked static world and other vehicles but not pedestrians. Promenade walkers yield by waiting at their current route point, so an NPC already overlapped by a car remained embedded.

`vehicle-pedestrian-contact.ts` shares the existing physical driving capsules (car center radius 1.16 m, nose/tail centers ±1.35 m and radius 0.9 m; bike radius 0.45 m), not the larger 3.2 m pedestrian yielding distance. Standing actors use radius 0.4 m and height 1.8 m. Existing down actors use a conservative radius 1.0 m and height 0.55 m, centered 0.9 m from their feet along the actual fallen axis (-cos heading, sin heading); this covers the rotated 1.8 m torso/head at headings 0° and 90°. No animation or state is changed. These simple dynamic bodies are gameplay approximations, not model-triangle collision.

Summons reject occupied body and immediate departure sweeps before creating a vehicle. Player driving sweeps the proposed motion in both directions and during steering, then stops at the last nonpenetrating pose. Short angular chords conservatively include the nose/tail arc sagitta. Height intervals exclude pedestrians on roads below the elevated deck. Existing overlaps recover using analytic boundary depth of the convex hull of the pedestrian-inflated physical discs. Exposed circular support arcs and common outer tangent lines define this hull without sampled angles. Only recovery uses this hull: it removes the small concave waist that otherwise traps a multi-frame centered escape. Newly entering actors still use the exact physical circle sweeps. The reviewer’s diagonal nose-deepening case is rejected; increased car-center distance alone is not a separation criterion. New pedestrians remain collision candidates during recovery.

`Actors.pedestrianBodies()` reads existing actor positions independently of rendering visibility. It does not change the six reserved identities, route progress, panic, down timers or animation. This fix prevents penetration; it does not make a stopped crowd find an alternate route. In a blocked narrow promenade, the driver may need to back away. No NPC hiding, geometry, collision-world exemption, or extra asset is used.
