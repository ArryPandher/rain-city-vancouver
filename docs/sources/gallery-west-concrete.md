# Gallery west terrace cast-concrete finish

The ordinary walking baseline `artifacts/local/robson-fill-public-baseline.png` shows staggered ashlar lines across the modern planted terrace fascia and supports at the left of the approach. Those lines came from the shared Gallery `foundation` material: 1.15 × 0.52 m staggered courses, including a joint-relief shader. The [Gallery's official 2023 exterior photograph](https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/VAG_exterior_2023_15-5-edited-1.jpg) instead shows rough cast-concrete fascia and piers beneath this planted edge. The photograph is retained for reference only in `artifacts/local/robson-realism-reference/vag-official-robson-exterior.jpg`; it is not distributed as a texture. It is not exposure- or colour-calibrated against the game.

This correction assigns a dedicated cast-concrete finish only to the existing west terrace hard members selected by `galleryWestTerraceBoxes()` with role `stone`: grounded supports, fascia, cap and planter. It does not change their positions, faces, collision or support. The existing floor, stairs, historic Gallery stone, glass, conifers and their atlas UVs retain their prior materials. No inherited ashlar shader is copied to the new material. The optional texture-library argument on `addGalleryFacade` is supplied by the real landmark loader; callers without that library retain their existing fallback.

## Existing licensed source and scale

The three maps derive from the already committed [Concrete Wall 009](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_009) by Charlotte Baglioni / Poly Haven, **CC0-1.0**, under `public/textures/cast-concrete`. No download is required. This is a generic concrete scan, not a scan of Vancouver or of the Gallery.

`node scripts/prepare-gallery-west-concrete.mjs` checks all three source MD5 hashes, extracts the proven joint- and tie-hole-free 200 × 200 px crop at `(300,380)`, and mirrors it into a 400 × 400 tile. The normal map's red/green components change sign on the corresponding reflected axes. Lossless WebP preserves the crop's diffuse texture. Source roughness is monotonically mapped into 0.82–0.98 for dry concrete. No wall seams, tie holes or artificial dirt are added.

The complete mirrored tile repeats every **0.6 m**, so its original 200 px quadrant covers 0.3 m and several-pixel marks become millimetre-scale pores. This scale and the 0.4 normal strength are authored plausible approximations, not surveyed aggregate sizes. Metric tangent-plane UVs are generated by the existing final Gallery batching path; independent samplers hold the scale. TextureLoader/OpenGL conventions are retained; these are not glTF tangentless UVs.

To isolate surface fidelity from broad brightness changes, the material multiplies the old foundation base colour by the ratio of old mineral-map and new concrete-map mean **linear RGB**. Thus the average base-colour × map response is retained per channel before lighting; the old procedural ashlar joint darkening is intentionally removed. The new roughness variation affects specular response, so this is not a pixel-identical luminance promise. The full diffuse variation remains in the crop. Lighting, HDR intensity, exposure and global weather are unchanged.

## Cost and verification

Three 400 px files total **298,038 bytes**. Uncompressed RGBA storage with mipmaps is approximately **2.44 MiB**, excluding engine/driver overhead. SharedTextureSources performs three source loads and handles pending image publication; materials own their sampler wrappers. The full Gallery factory gains **one batch (9 to 10)** with **no additional triangles**; its current total remains **32,192 triangles**. Geometry and exact surface-selection tests compare the final batched triangles, not just helper parameters. They also verify metric UV distances, image readiness for multiple consumers, sampler disposal, committed image/source hashes, colour variation and mean response.

Thirteen focused material, actual-GLB facade and west-terrace/source/contact tests passed, and full TypeScript validation passed. Native walking comparison is still required from the same arrival position and lighting as the baseline. Acceptance should depend on concrete grain and absence of false stone courses at close range; this does not repair the broader source wing's simplified architecture or claim that darker masonry is inherently more realistic.

## Native review, 2026-11-16

Frozen candidate14cd8d9 passed the production build. In the existing Codex tab, the arrival comparison used exactly the earlier camera/player, FOV62, south180 daylight, hemisphere1.15 and exposure1.08. False staggered courses are absent from the selected modern fascia; the replacement reads as a continuous concrete surface. A normal lateral walk to `[-77.26,0,48.44]` also inspected the close supports. The historic wing's separate stone joints, floor and architecture remain unchanged. This is accepted as a bounded source-accuracy correction, not a major overall realism advance.

All105 shared texture sources were ready with no pending/failed URLs, and no warnings/errors were captured from the new bundle.
