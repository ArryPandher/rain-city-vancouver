# Canada Place approach support

The bounded rendered support uses the mapped Howe/Canada Place road corridor and the shared `canada-place-site.ts` height profile. Source IDs, official access references and the elevation interpretation are recorded in [the grade plan](canada-place-grade-plan.md) and [access evidence](canada-place-access.md). The 7.88 m finished arrival datum matches the supplied Canada Place model; inferred grades, sidewalk widths, kerb dimensions and the 10 m entry connector are integration estimates, not surveyed street measurements.

`canada-place-approach.ts` triangulates the corridor with actual building holes and subdivides tops to follow the shared profile. Structural pavement is 0.065 m above baseline, below the existing road asphalt. Sidewalks and the bounded entry connector finish at baseline +0.15 m. Retaining faces extend to the previous terrain datum; they do not raise whole adjacent buildings or claim specific real door locations. The source pier is excluded by the site helper, so its original geometry supplies the deck. Unioned mapped carriageways omit internal junction kerbs; mapped crossing paths and the explicit arrival connector have kerb openings. Four bounded material batches use existing concrete, stone and dark painted metal.

Affected mapped neighbours include w363651936, w138256216, w208599553 and w143683980. Their centroids remain on the original building datum. The parent integration suppresses generated ground-floor shops/doors when their outward sidewalk sample is more than 0.6 m above that datum, avoiding buried invented entrances without moving historic or commercial masses. Authentic entrance positions remain unverified.

`canada-place-paths.ts` subtracts the exact source pier footprint from generic pedestrian ribbons triangle by triangle. It interpolates UVs barycentrically and resamples remaining terrain heights, preserving exterior path segments while leaving the existing deck and closed hotel/hall surface unobscured.

Checks use the actual mapped road/building fixture and source pier outline: building interiors and original pier receive no duplicate pavement; connector datum agrees with the original deck; all top normals point up; interpolation follows the shared grade; retaining walls reach the old terrain; batching is bounded. Path checks verify a real arrival-boundary cut, exterior area retention, UVs, heights and an unchanged unrelated ribbon. Native gameplay/visual acceptance belongs to the parent task and is not implied by these CPU geometry tests.


The added outer-edge guards are explicitly estimated game-safety geometry, not claimed real Vancouver rail layouts. `canada-place-guards.ts` shares base/top heights and endpoints with collision: 1.05 m painted steel rails, posts no more than 2 m apart, inset 0.12 m from the platform edge. They appear only where the outward old terrain is more than 0.75 m lower. Building contacts, all polygon holes, the explicit arrival connector, supported source-deck continuation and matched western terrain remain unguarded. The renderer uses the same segments for grounded posts and top bars in one additional batch. Retaining wall UVs now use edge distance and world height at four metres per repeat, avoiding vertically collapsed texture coordinates.
