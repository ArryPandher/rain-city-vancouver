# Canada Place upper west-promenade guard

The cached Juan Tanus photograph in BC Museums Association *Roundup*, Fall2012 p21 ([publication](https://museum.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/253-roundup_fall2012.pdf)), and the historical Rayner west section show a raised pale guard with posts and horizontal rails above the stepped glazing. Local references: `museum-west-photo.png` and `rayner-section-west-crop.png` in `artifacts/local/canada-place-pedestrian-references/`. They establish the architectural type, not surveyed dimensions or a current access entitlement. The photographs are reference only; no image texture is redistributed.

`addCanadaPlaceUpperGuard(parent, material?)` appends one source-local mesh under the unchanged landmark group offset+.08Y. The coping sits on the existing roof at local17.65; total guard height1.08m, square posts140mm, top rail110×80mm, two intermediate70mm rails, and coping280×140mm are explicit photographic estimates. Post bays follow the existing source-registered stations with a maximum1.9m gap. End returns close back to source wall at the first/last stations. Those exact termination details are authored approximations; the cached images do not establish each endpoint connection.

The path follows the actual roof perimeter: central upper-curve head, the projecting upright end fascias, and transverse links at the existing22/81.10m transitions. It does not bridge the inset roof steps with floating rail sections. Upper access remains closed. Ground footprints, source selection, doors, glazing, stay assemblies and public route are unchanged.

Cost: one additional material/draw batch,4308triangles and301560bytes of position/normal/UV/index buffers. Three targeted tests cover both terminal returns, both fascia/curve transitions, downward rays from every coping segment to the actual generated facade roof/end geometry, finite unit normals and the unchanged minimum roof datum. TypeScript passes. Native walking-height views at the southern and middle concourse show the attached upper edge and returns; this does not establish precise current guard detailing.
