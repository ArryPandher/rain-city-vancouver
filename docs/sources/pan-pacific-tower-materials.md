# Pan Pacific tower material and facade evidence — 2026-11-15

This factory is scoped to the replaced hotel tower. It does not modify the completed Howe podium, west junction, sails, shared city palette, lighting or existing material instances. Native acceptance belongs to the integrated tower review; these authored values are not measured building optics.

## References and required facade hierarchy

Original [MCM Handford20](https://www.mcmparchitects.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Handford-MCM-CanadaPlace-20-WEB.jpg) (2048×1713) shows a broad near glazed grid beside a pale vertical rounded edge. A narrow glazed corner return separates it from the long facade. The latter's pale punched-window field is **stepped/L-shaped**, not one uniform horizontal division: the near portion has roughly five visible punched rows under the crown, with glass below; the far/nose portion continues in pale punched windows down toward the podium. Original image controls for the pale region are approximately `(940,328)→(1295,574)→(1316,946)→(1120,882)→(1118,661)→(940,541)`, clockwise, with curved ends and ±5 px interpretation tolerance. These are photo pixels, never metres.

At image y610 the near-face light vertical grid is around x`[571,594,607,630,652,665,688,711,724,747,770,784,807,830,844,868]`; its 15 visible intervals have a recurring wider/narrower/wider hierarchy. At x700 the stronger horizontal frame rows are y`[338,381,424,467,510,553,596,639,682,725,768]`, with thin intermediate transoms around y`[358,401,444,487,530,573,616,659,702,745]`. These finite samples do not establish a floor count or hidden-face schedule. Geometry should represent slim frames and actual facade zones, not wrap 19 broad source concrete bands around every face.

[Handford16](https://www.mcmparchitects.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Handford-MCM-CanadaPlace-16-WEB.jpg) (2048×1549) resolves the rounded nose: predominantly pale punched windows, setback/terrace breaks near original pixels `(665,444)` and `(680,665)`, and a thin dark glazed crown from about `(650,98)` to `(650,148)`. Its central x≈665 section resolves 11 visible window rows, which are not surveyed storeys. [Handford18](https://www.mcmparchitects.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Handford-MCM-CanadaPlace-18-WEB.jpg) confirms shallow rounded-corner apertures, thin paired-window centre mullions, restrained panel joints and real terrace depth. It also shows curtains, but no photographic or procedural curtain texture is introduced by this material pass.

All three photographs were inspected at original resolution from the cached MCM references. Their upload path is 2025/09; capture dates, photometric calibration, actual material specifications and exact metric dimensions were not verified. They remain reference only and are not shipped as textures. Detailed point/control interpretation is in the local `artifacts/local/pan-pacific-tower-facade-trace.md`; plan, roof shape and metric geometry are owned by the separate architecture pass.

## Runtime surface contract

`createPanPacificTowerMaterials(library)` returns four opaque shared roles (`concrete`, `frame`, `backing`, `roof`) and `glassPair`. Warm pale concrete `#d7d3c7` shares the podium's mineral family while independently owning all sampler wrappers. Slim frames `#b9bbaa` have roughness 0.34/metalness 0.28; dark closed cavity backing `#253339` has roughness 0.93; matte crown/roof finish `#414947` has roughness 0.86. These are authored daylight estimates. No material has emissive fill, baked pane grid or randomized interior lights.

Mineral maps reuse the committed `public/textures/canada-place-plinth/{color,normal,roughness}.webp` crop derived from Charlotte Baglioni / Poly Haven's [Concrete Wall 009](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_009), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Exact provenance/preparation remains in `public/textures/canada-place-plinth/SOURCES.json`. This is generic mineral surface detail, not a Pan Pacific scan. No source downloads or licence changes. Color is sRGB; normal/roughness remain linear. Geometry supplies metre UVs; all maps repeat at estimated 2 m, use matched transforms and restrained normal strength 0.08. Roughness multiplier 1 preserves the prepared map's approximately 0.74–0.86 range. No additional panel-joint shader competes with architectural apertures.

Glass uses the existing hotel's linear-HDR extinction/reflection pair, independently calibrated to channel throughput `[0.55,0.74,0.80]`, IOR 1.5, roughness 0.12, environment intensity 0.85. The first draw attenuates the already rendered closed cavity with RGB/Fresnel transmission, the second adds dielectric specular reflection. This makes the glass's hue and reflection depend on actual backing/environment instead of diffuse blue paint. Metalness, transmission and clearcoat remain zero; there is no new capture, light or refraction buffer. The inherited near-building no-fog approximation and alpha-preserving blending remain unchanged.

`createPanPacificTowerGlazingMesh(geometry, glassPair)` clones the caller's indexed geometry and assigns ordered attenuation/reflection groups. Share the pair across coherent faces, never a material per pane. Root assigns the environment to `pan-pacific-tower-glass`; a podium probe may only approximate the tower's elevated surroundings, so native review decides its adequacy. Four opaque submissions plus two per coherent glass face-group is the material budget before shadows; actual draw/triangle counts are reported by integration. Keep outward normals, real reveal depth and opaque closed backing at joins. Avoid coincident duplicate glass layers or flattening the ordered groups.
