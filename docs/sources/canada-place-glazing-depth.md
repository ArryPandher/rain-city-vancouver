# Canada Place shallow upper glazing structure

Reference: Juan Tanus's west-concourse photograph in BC Museums Association *Roundup*, Fall 2012 p 21 ([publication](https://museum.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/253-roundup_fall2012.pdf)), cached as `museum-west-photo.png`, and the historical Rayner west section `rayner-section-west-crop.png`. These show pale subdivisions and framed curved glazing rather than uninterrupted opaque metal-like sheets. They support the architectural type; the following cavity dimensions and pane subdivision spacing are explicitly authored approximations, not surveyed construction details or a depiction of the actual interior.

Curved exterior coordinates, analytic normals and longitudinal station registration are unchanged. Existing longitudinal mullions now use a dedicated pale upper-frame material, while intermediate rails divide each quarter-round into four bands (approximately 0.9 m arc length). Ground door materials and geometry remain separate.

A closed structural backing sits 400 mm inward along the pane's analytic normal. Dark muted backing faces and pale side reveals provide real view-dependent separation behind the material agent's thin-sheet transparent glass. Reveals close each longitudinal bay and the ends of each curved tier; tessellation inside the tier introduces no extra opaque crossbars. Identical adjoining reveal surfaces are deduplicated. The backing is a shallow closed visual proxy, not a furnished or accessible concourse. Exact cavity depth, backing colour and structure are estimates subject to native visual calibration.

Source selection still removes exactly 10 reviewed concrete,100 glass and 480 trim triangles. Existing roof/back/transition closures stay intact. End-cap glass is a separate opaque material; it is not made transparent without equivalent backing. Only curved upper glass preserves its factory transparency, depth-write, shader and shadow flags. The ground-door fallback stays opaque, and any explicitly supplied ground-door material is retained.

Total returned facade geometry: 61084 triangles in 8 material batches, including 9560 backing/reveal triangles in one batch. Splitting opaque end caps adds the second additional batch over the former 6. The existing guard is unchanged and remains a separate mesh. There are no added textures, reflection captures, playable interiors or collision/route changes.

Seventeen targeted facade/guard/material tests pass, plus TypeScript. Tests retain actual decoded source GLB geometry and verify normal and both oblique rays behind transparent panes first meet the new shallow backing, not residual source walls. Existing exterior curvature, roof/guard contact, source selection, source paving height and public-route tests remain passing. Final transparency/depth appearance requires the parent's native browser comparison; these checks do not establish photographic parity.

Native review found full-depth pale returns too heavy. Only the front 100 mm rebate now remains pale; the remaining 300 mm shares the muted backing colour. The 400 mm closed depth and exterior coordinates are unchanged. Both portions are created after deduplicating the complete shared return, preserving one physical wall between adjacent bays. A targeted geometry test casts rays at six depths across the split, verifies pale vertices stop at 100 mm, confirms uninterrupted coverage, and checks that shared bays do not duplicate either colour band. Splitting the returns adds 3428 triangles without additional materials or draws.
