# Canada Place daylight mast and stay refinement

2026-11-14. Bounded original exterior detail around the existing five-sail landmark. No global lighting change, ray tracing, new interiors, public-route change or new game texture.

The directly inspected cached architectural photograph `artifacts/local/canada-material-reference.jpg` shows the promenade side with substantial pale mast shafts, fittings and long straight tension stays against the sails. [DA Architects' project page](https://www.da-architects.ca/projects/canada-place-pan-pacific-hotel/) identifies its northeast-facing view as [CanadaPlacePanPaciffic_3.jpg](https://www.da-architects.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CanadaPlacePanPaciffic_3.jpg). The project page was read through web retrieval; direct HTTP requests to its current page/image returned403, so no fresh image download is claimed. The cached photograph supplied actual visual evidence. [Zeidler's project account](https://zeidler.com/projects/canada-place/) describes the tensile roof and multi-level promenades; it does not provide surveyed rigging dimensions.

The source GLB and [Codriver geometry](https://github.com/codriver-io/codriver-3d-assets/blob/main/src/peregrine/landmarks/top-cities/canada-place/geometry.js) already contain ten mast boxes: two edge endpoints on each of five sails. Their exact local anchors are `p(1,0)` and `p(1,1)` from the source sail function, equivalently ring vertices0 and3 in [canada-place-site.js](https://github.com/codriver-io/codriver-3d-assets/blob/main/src/peregrine/landmarks/top-cities/canada-place/canada-place-site.js). The five rings are retained with provenance in `src/world/canada-place-rigging-map.json`. Codriver geometry remains CC BY4.0, with geographic placement © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The narrow loader hook removes only the original ten mast boxes from the merged steel primitive before adding tapered pale shafts, shaft collars and a straight tension stay per mast. A test decodes the actual committed GLB, including its normalized/quantized positions and node transforms, and verifies exactly120 original triangles are removed. Rails and other original steel remain. The new stay endpoints meet shaft fittings and physical sockets at the existing membrane valley; all new geometry stays above17.6m. No blocking objects or changed pier/roof extents are introduced at walking level.

Taper diameters, collar dimensions and chosen straight stay layout are authored interpretations, not surveyed engineering drawings. The stay's low end uses the source membrane edge valley at u=.42; its chord clears the curved membrane edge. These details aim to make daylight sails read as supported architectural fabric rather than isolated spikes. They do not establish photographic parity or structural-engineering fidelity. Review both the ordinary promenade approach and the harbour view before accepting them.

The addition uses two shared opaque materials and fewer than4,000 triangles, with no extra lights, reflection probes or runtime animation. Tests also check finite geometry, height bounds and physical anchor sockets. The parent owns final browser visual acceptance.
