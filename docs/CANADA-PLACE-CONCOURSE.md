# Canada Place entrance and concourse

This checkpoint develops the small Canada Place gameplay region. GTA VI / AAA realism remains the goal; the scene is still an architectural approximation with substantial visual and performance work remaining.

The former flat west hall frontage is now a continuous 103.1 m envelope with three stepped curved tiers, ground glazing, physical frames, a recessed upper terrace and closed glazed end sections. Architectural sections, a ground-level photograph and registered aerial imagery establish the hall/terrace/glass ordering. The 14.4 m central projection and tapered ends are explicit estimates. The separate 51.9 m hotel entrance has a quarter-ellipse conservatory, closed ends and a grounded mineral base. Source sails, original vertices, pier elevations and upper hotel remain intact.

Both expanded envelopes are closed collision volumes. The retained walking route has more than 0.95 m clearance from the concourse footprint, including its frame allowance; the collision system then applies its 0.55 m facade margin and player radius. Tests intersect the actual decoded source geometry, not a duplicate mock floor. Initial integration caught a constriction, and the concourse projection was reduced from 14.8 m to 14.4 m. Sill/glass coplanar overlap and faceted glass normals were also corrected during review.

The paving now uses dedicated source-derived CC0 mineral maps, preserving its mean colour while restoring grain, a roughness range of 0.75–0.85 and stronger mineral normals. Filtered colour seams and analytic joint gradients avoid undefined higher-order GLSL derivatives. Three 512 px WebP maps add 170,112 bytes. Their raw inputs, hashes, preparation and licence are in `public/textures/canada-place-paving/SOURCES.json`; reproduce with `scripts/prepare-canada-place-paving.mjs`.

Two 128 px static reflection captures use the existing scene-probe system for the new glass. They capture fixed harbour/city geometry during loading and weather refresh, omit moving actors and the receiving surfaces, and do not add continuous six-face rendering during normal play. They are approximate local environment maps, not ray tracing or exact planar reflections.

Distant street crowns now use their existing layered source canopy when individual leaf geometry is too small on screen. Nearby and zoomed-in crowns retain individual leaves; hysteresis stabilizes the transition. Restoration tests verify that the original crown and trunk matrices return before detailed slots disappear. A matched stationary downtown view went from 24 to 8 individual-leaf trees while retaining visible silhouettes. The 20-second samples measured 23.93 fps before and 25.16 fps after; p95 was 50.5 / 50.8 ms. Traffic and NPC state differed, so this is not an isolated or sustained speed-up claim.

## Evidence and limits

Sources: [hotel entrance](sources/canada-place-entrance-canopy.md), [hall concourse](sources/canada-place-west-facade.md), [paving](sources/canada-place-paving.md). Official Rockstar stills remain the art benchmark; Vancouver references establish architecture. No Rockstar imagery or reference photographs are distributed as game assets.

Longitudinal tier-height variation, exact endpoint junctions, ground stay-anchor plinths/full-height rigging, documented furnishings, convincing contact/weathering, character animation, water and wider city massing remain unfinished. Opaque upper fascias do not reproduce the photographed changing tier heights. Local reflections improve environmental response but do not establish photographic parity. The five-minute/1080p performance gate remains unmet.
