# Canada Place ground stays and recessed promenade

This checkpoint continues the small Canada Place gameplay region. GTA VI / AAA realism remains the target; the scene is still well below photographic parity. Vancouver section drawings and ground photography guide architecture, while the Rockstar references set the art benchmark.

Five protective anchor bodies now connect paired full-height cables to the registered west mast heads. Bevelled trunnion caps, pin faces and turned barrel ends give the nearby fittings physical depth. The module uses three material batches and 13,470 triangles. Dedicated 512 px CC0 mineral maps add 90,894 bytes. Their source grain, restrained normals and mild ground exposure avoid the facade's block courses and form ties. See [geometry sources and estimates](sources/canada-place-ground-stays.md) and `public/textures/canada-place-plinth/SOURCES.json`.

The historical section establishes that the ground doors sit behind the lowest curved canopy edge. The new ground glazing is recessed two metres at the central bays, with a closed soffit and return ends. Upper opaque fascia sections are now upright rather than another repeated curve. Longitudinal transition stations and transverse dimensions remain estimates; the full changing tier-height pattern still needs refinement.

The support bodies sit partly beneath this overhang. The walking/driving line passes on their waterside without enlarging the source pier or disabling rails/water protection. Actual movement tests sample the continuous route every 0.1 m, including the car centre and nose/tail collision capsules. The minimum clearance beyond the car capsules is 0.270 m. Exact body footprints are height-gated, and ground glazing has a separate footprint from the overhead envelope. See [route and collision evidence](sources/canada-place-ground-stay-route.md).

Native review exposed a car-summoning failure beside the north anchor: the centre was clear but the nose/tail touched a base. Placement now checks the same three capsules used by driving, checks their support and water classification on the player's level, and searches nearby smaller offsets. It also checks a three-metre departure corridor for cars (one metre for bikes), so a clear parked pose does not immediately trap the vehicle on throttle. A regression test reproduces the observed position before checking the resolved placement. Crowded or unsupported candidates fail without placing an unusable vehicle.

Geometry availability is per loaded world instance. The loader enables only refinements actually added to the scene; missing or rejected refinements cannot leave invisible blockers. Ground west stays supersede the earlier roof-valley approximation when the facade preflight succeeds, retaining east attachments and all mast shafts.
