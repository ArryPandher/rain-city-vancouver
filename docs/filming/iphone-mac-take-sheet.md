## Recording order

2. **Mac:** Open [Rain City](https://rain-city-vancouver.vercel.app/) and let startup finish. Use Settings → Pacific daylight and Balanced; choose Explore on foot. Press C until the toast says Chase camera or Close camera. Record a short sample first and replay it to check motion and audio.

## Screen routes beside the keyboard

- **Canada Place:** On foot, M → Canada Place. Use the broad entrance approach near the crosswalk for the sails. Avoid the narrow hotel-column path ending at a railing. For the car clip, summon with V at the arrival street, tap W to around 20–30 km/h, brake with Space before the crossing/raised apron, then E to exit. Record car entry and driving as separate clips if the camera move is awkward.
- **Harbour Green:** On foot, M → Harbour Green Park. Head toward the water and down the broad Bute steps. Keep some path/stairs in the frame so the movement reads; finish on the fountain and harbour/mountain view.
- **Gallery:** On foot, M → Robson Square. Frame the entrance facing Robson Square. Record the approach, stairs, and terrace turn separately.
- **Weapons:** On foot, 1 equips the pistol; right mouse aims, left mouse fires, Q holsters. Pick a clear backdrop so the shot reads. Do not use R in the car: it recovers the vehicle.
- **Mouse:** Click the game to capture the pointer; Escape releases it. C cycles camera modes; it is not a camera-selection menu.
